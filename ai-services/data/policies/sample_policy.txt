
# Example Health Insurance Policy
Policy ID: DEMO-001

## Hospitalization Coverage
The example policy covers eligible inpatient hospitalization expenses up to $5,000 per policy year, subject to the policy terms and exclusions.

## Required Documents
A hospitalization claim must include an itemized hospital bill, discharge summary, proof of identity, and a completed claim form.

## Outpatient Consultations
Outpatient consultations are not covered under this example policy.

## Prescription Medicines
Prescription medicines purchased outside an eligible inpatient hospitalization are not covered under this example policy.

## Chronic Conditions
Routine outpatient treatment for chronic conditions, including diabetes management, is not covered under this example policy. Insulin purchased for routine home use is not covered.

## Diagnosis-Specific Limits
The example policy does not define separate coverage limits for individual diagnoses. Eligible inpatient hospitalization expenses share the annual limit of $5,000.

## Exclusions
Cosmetic procedures that are not medically necessary are excluded under this example policy.

## Claim Review
If a required document is missing, request the missing document before completing the claim assessment. A missing document or unusual claim pattern alone does not prove fraud.

## Fraud Review
Potential fraud indicators must be reviewed by an authorized human reviewer. An unusual claim pattern is a reason for further review, not proof of fraud.

## Policy Limitations
Coverage depends on eligibility, the applicable policy terms, exclusions, and the remaining annual limit. If this example policy does not specify coverage for a service, the system must state that the policy does not provide enough information to determine coverage.
